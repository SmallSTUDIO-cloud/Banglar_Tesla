# Vehicle physics gap closure — browser verification (2026-10-30)

This is a **bounded arcade-contact pass**, not an AAA-completion or device-performance certification. Original HEAD: `2dc02d776b2cdb520f53471f08fcb51e6fdf251e`, branch `arena/1cbc19eb-banglar-tesla`. The working tree was dirty before this pass. Nothing was staged, committed, pushed, reset, cleaned, or rebased.

## Fourteen acceptance answers

1. **Eight NPC classes contacted?** Yes, in 80 scripted real-`Game.update` browser cases: car, bus, truck, van, CNG, easybike, motorbike, NPC rickshaw × ten scenarios. Every case recorded at least one actual controller hit. These are controlled fixtures, not human driving.
2. **Low/moderate front impact?** Yes. Front-moderate contact travel: car 1.10 m, bus 0.05 m, truck 0.02 m, van 1.00 m, CNG/easybike/motorbike/rickshaw 1.10 m. Low-speed results are in `artifacts/contact-matrix/results.json`.
3. **Rear and side impact?** Yes, collisions registered. Rear produced zero push travel, intentionally; side produced 0.24 m bounded lateral travel for car/van/light classes, nearly none for bus/truck. This is not a full rigid-body lateral solver.
4. **Repeated, moving, braking, junction, mixed traffic, release?** All ten scenarios were exercised per class. Moving contact produced only 0–0.04 m travel; junction nudge was often zero under the existing turn protection. Mixed traffic does not prove general dense-traffic safety; its staged second agent is controlled.
5. **Before/after velocities, displacement and contact duration measured?** Yes. Each JSON row contains before speed/position, first-contact speed, end-of-contact speed, push/lateral travel, net displacement, hit count, first-to-last-hit duration and step samples. Duration is the span between sampled hits, not a continuous manifold contact timer.
6. **Sustained push?** Yes for stationary aligned light/medium targets with throttle in free roam: capped at 1.1 m contact travel (van about 1.0 m), with capped 0.24 m lateral drift. Heavy bus/truck travel remains small. No claim that continuous pushing can exceed the encounter cap or work at a junction; steering/approach and cooldown matter.
7. **Mass/direction/speed loss and no launches?** The throttle-only low-relative-speed retention is disabled for bus/truck, side/rear, and active races. The NPC's response scales by kind and contact direction. In 80 staged cases max NPC one-step movement was 0.796 m (the large step needs scrutiny; it is below the fixture's 2 m fail threshold). This does not prove absence of every clipping/launch in free driving.
8. **Recovery and route resumption?** 80/80 final states finite and within the fixture's lane-width bound, 79/80 attained >0.5 m/s within 3 seconds after the player was removed; car/moving did not. The lane and `s` are recorded, but full end-to-end route completion, building penetration and all-world intersections are not established. Recovery is PARTIAL.
9. **Potholes for player and NPC?** The authored eight potholes are present; browser road fixture measured 0.065 m center depth and a drive. Three unit tests verify wheel response vs speed/miss/suspension and mass-scaled NPC impulse. NPC per-class **live crossing at each authored hole** was not measured; mark partial.
10. **High-impact visual gaps fixed without geometry bloat?** No additional art/geometry was added in this pass. Existing graphics were inspected through the 18-frame manifest and existing render/visual tests; visible stylization and the inconclusive rain screenshot are not an AAA-quality signoff.
11. **Frustum and distance visibility?** Independently checked with real renderer callbacks and a displaced world mesh entering/leaving a frustum. The before/after fixed-view render counts are identical: front 175 calls/578,845 triangles, left 128/535,946, behind 52/263,132. Distance checks and settings/weather states are in `artifacts/visibility-gap-after/audit.json`. No occlusion-culling claim.
12. **NPC LOD and world chunks?** Seven kinds × 26 near→far→near live traffic LOD samples recorded in `artifacts/visibility-transitions/`; eight-kind rendered near/far vehicle fixture passed. World-chunk bounds, front/back render counts, and 12 repeated show/hide resource cycles (279 geometries, 19 textures, 67 programs, 37 fleet children before/after) passed. This is visibility/LOD/chunk evidence, not occlusion.
13. **18 visual scenes and regressions?** `artifacts/aaa-visual-matrix.json` indexes 18 actual browser PNGs, including contact→continued push→release→recovery, menu, hero, chase, night, rain, passenger, DRMC and density, plus LOD. Not all scenes have formal image-diff assertions. Typecheck, build, 221/221 unit tests, browser 21/21, mobile-browser 13/13, UI 33/33 passed. Traffic-advanced, vehicle-quality, pothole browser, weather-lighting and rain browser checks passed. A combined race/visibility command and separate night command timed out; do not count those as passes. Prior race unit tests passed, but current live-race run printed a result then timed out. The 80-case matrix passed after per-kind isolation; one preceding cross-kind run failed for rickshaw, so stability is not fully proven.

Neither JDK nor `adb` is in PATH; no official SDK/platform/build-tools installation, Gradle APK build, install or physical-device test was done.
