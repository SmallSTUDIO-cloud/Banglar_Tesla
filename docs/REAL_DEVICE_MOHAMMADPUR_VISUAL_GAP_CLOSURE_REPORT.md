The original browser-before files cannot be recovered, and **there is no reproducible matched browser before/after pixel set now**. The fixed cameras labelled `former-campus` and `former-campus-interior` in the capture *fixture* now look into newly placed buildings and are **obstructed, not useful visual comparisons**. ## Scope, causes and changes

- **Map audit:** Existing authored road nodes/segments, junction disks, footpath clearance, place anchors and sector batching were retained. Earlier street-front infill left substantial *interior* empty ground; a frontage-only pass could not address it. `src/world/city.ts` now places bounded, oriented compact block-interior buildings by ranking eligible gaps, with sector and total caps, collision footprints, road/footpath/junction and existing-building exclusion, varied 2–4-storey height, sills/thresholds/roof elements and shared batched materials. An actual browser city fixture reports **35 frontage infills, 160 interior buildings, 19 small courts, one pocket plaza**. These are in-game procedural placements, not surveyed parcel footprints. Existing legitimate courts and open space remain; not every blank area is filled.
- **Street-level design:** Existing colored plaster/brick, shutters, shop signs, awnings, cables, lamps, stalls, courtyards, planted areas and NPC pathing remain; the interior and frontage masses provide more layered coverage. The shared NPC-bus detail geometry gained rear glazing, hatch, tail-lamp and reflector shapes without per-bus draw calls. The hero pedal rickshaw, NPC classes, wheel phases, traffic logic and controls are retained. This is a stylized low-poly visual pass, **not photorealism or surveyed building reproduction**.
- **Road damage:** Eight authored pothole coordinates/radii continue to drive `potholeDepthAt` and terrain contact. A shared indexed, irregular-edged surface mesh adds a sloped lip and dark center at those exact locations. Fixing its index format was necessary: the first after attempt generated indexed/nonindexed geometry-merge errors and missing roads, so those invalid frames were rejected. The final 39-scene pass has zero diagnostics and zero GL errors. The visible shallow basin is a surface illusion laid over the continuous road ribbon: its **visible slope does not equal the full 0.065-unit physical center depression**; do not present it as a cut into the road mesh. A real carved asphalt aperture remains a visual limitation.
- **Weather, time and occupied windows:** Existing rain, puddles, spray, night lamps/sky and road fill are preserved. Rain subtly darkens shared plaster, brick and paving uniforms and lowers shared shutter roughness. **Buildings now turn on a sparse, deterministic selection of warm-colored upper-floor window and balcony panes as night falls**, fading in through dusk and off again by daylight. They are 15 cullable, sector-batched meshes with a single shared transparent material, not actual point lights, room interiors, added textures, or shadow casters. Dry, rain, night and storm fixtures were captured; a configured clear-after-rain scene is not a proven live transition on a phone.
- **Retired landmark removal:** DRMC-specific scene geometry, its public-road access exemptions, protected boundary, place/POI destination, minimap destination button, HUD labels, navigation state, localization entries and active landmark-specific scripts/tests were removed. General Mohammadpur road routes, map, missions, career, traffic and ambient pedestrians remain. `Scrapbook.fromJSON` filters old `landmark:drmc` records while retaining unrelated records and earnings. Remaining `landmark:drmc` in source is only that migration literal; browser test checks search for forbidden runtime identifiers. Historical reports in `docs/` describe previous builds and are *not* current map instructions.

### Research and interpretation

The authored network uses prior OSM-derived roads in `src/world/mohammadpurData.ts`; it is a game-scale graph, not a surveyed parcel or present-day storefront inventory. Background sources used to choose a *mix* of market spine, quieter lanes, walkable courts and restrained vegetation: [UNESCAP 2019 Dhaka mobility assessment](https://www.unescap.org/resources/suti-mobility-assessment-report-dhaka) (historical city-wide context, not 2026 speeds); [Debnath et al., 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8190833/) (pedestrian/environment factors, not local counts); [Rahman & Nilufar, 2024](https://doiserbia.nb.rs/img/doi/1450-569X/2024/1450-569X2452001R.pdf) (Dhaka nodes and pedestrian/transit integration); [Kabir, 2023](https://ijsmr.in/doc/ijsmr06_103.pdf) (Town Hall bazaar/planning, redevelopment proposal **not** proof of built plaza); [Bangladesh Tourism Board, undated](https://beautifulbangladesh.gov.bd/district-destination/dhaka/heritage/103) (Sat Gambuj location and historic context); and [Razi, 25 July 2026](https://www.thedailystar.net/slow-reads/unheard-voices/news/republic-the-footpath-4231611) (attributed qualitative account of a market road, residential alley and shaded pedestrian lane). None establishes exact 2026 business names, densities, building footprints, phone performance or the former site's real-world land use. `docs/DHAKA_REALITY_NOTES.md` holds the older research context. One attempted World Bank PDF returned HTTP 403 and was not used.

Captures use matching conditions *within the final pass* for quality/weather/time/camera variants, but not a recoverable earlier browser baseline.

These are software-browser draw submissions, not Android GPU timings.
