## Cultural and legal notes

* Rickshaw painting is a living Bangladeshi art tradition; the panels here are **original
  interpretations** in that spirit, drawn from scratch, not reproductions of existing panels.
* “Tesla” appears only as part of this game's joke title. No Tesla, Inc. logo, typeface, product
  silhouette or trade dress is used or imitated. The emblem is a rickshaw wheel with a floral
  accent.
* No political slogans, religious content, real tragedies, real people, or real company branding
  appear in the street dressing.
* All passenger dialogue is original, short, respectful, and stored as editable keys in
  `src/core/i18n.ts`.

If you add an external asset later, record **author, source URL, licence, required attribution and
where it is used** in this file before merging.
